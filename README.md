I'm a Frappe / ERPNext developer and technical consultant focused on building reliable business applications — custom ERP solutions, integrations, and full-stack web systems.

My work covers the complete development lifecycle: requirements, architecture, development, customization, deployment, and ongoing optimization. I enjoy turning complex business requirements into scalable, maintainable, practical software.

- 🏢 Building and customizing ERPNext / Frappe applications
